getveraai.com · audit and redesign brief18 Nov 2026 · Impeccable audit + critique

The copy is strong. The design hides it.

Vera's homepage has specific, credible writing and a sharp mechanism: matching purchase orders, challans, store issues and the BOQ until the missing bags show up. The visual layer doesn't do it justice. A 4-second newspaper intro sits in front of the hero, the styling is a common AI-generated template, and too much of the small text is hard to read. This brief audits the current site and then lays out a full redesign, with a second market added: contractors in Dubai and the UAE.

What to fix, in order

Ranked by impact on the visit. P0 blocks the visitor, P1 hurts trust or breaks WCAG AA, P2 adds friction, P3 is polish.

P0

Remove the newspaper intro

Every visit opens on a blurred fake broadsheet that pans around one highlighted fragment: "firms now using Vera AI save", "a new report on Vera AI said". The fragments stop mid-sentence, so it reads like a glitch. The made-up headlines ("Infra Spending Set to Double by 2030", "RERA Tightens Rules on Project Delays") look like news coverage Vera doesn't have, and a skeptical contractor will notice. It also frames Vera as a media brand. It holds the headline back for 4.0 s on desktop and mobile.

Fix: Delete it. Don't replace it with another intro. The new hero carries the one motion moment, and only after the content is already readable (see the rubber stamp in the hero mock below).

P1

The styling looks like an AI template

Near-black ground with one vermilion accent (#FF5B1F), a cream band (#F4F1EC), Geist for everything, and an orange second line on five headlines in a row ("accounted for.", "walks off site.", "run on it.", "build India.", "from day one."). Each section is a sheet with 40px rounded top corners sliding over the last. Impeccable's detector flags this exact combination as one of the most common generated looks. Visitors who have seen many AI-built sites will notice it, and it hides how specific the product is.

Fix: Build a new visual world from the contractor's own paperwork (Direction A below). Use one accent per screen, not one per headline.

P1

Small labels fail contrast

52 text nodes fall below WCAG AA. Examples: "MATERIAL · MONEY · PROOF" at 2.62:1, the ledger sub-labels "PO-2291" and "3 trucks" at 2.67:1, "FOR CONTRACTORS DOING ₹50–500 CR A YEAR" at 3.46:1, and all 10px mono captions in the desk mock-ups at about 3.5:1. Your buyer is often a 50-year-old owner reading on a phone in sunlight.

Fix: No text under 12px. Secondary text should be a solid color at 4.5:1 or better, not an opacity step.

P1

Stacked desk cards look broken while scrolling

The four desks stack as sticky cards. Earlier cards stay visible as dimmed slivers behind the current one. Mid-scroll, the Site Record Desk shows an empty grey panel where its document preview should be (screenshot below). On mobile this section accounts for most of the page's 13,800px.

Fix: Replace it with one desk switcher: four tabs and one large live document.

P1

A label above every section

"NEW", "THE LEAK", "WHAT VERA DOES", "HOW WE WORK", "ABOUT US", "START HERE", plus a second tracked label on the right ("MATERIAL · MONEY · PROOF"). Impeccable bans this outright: when every heading gets a label, the labels stop meaning anything, and they're the lowest-contrast text on the page.

Fix: Delete them all. The headings are good enough to stand alone.

P2

The hero video is generic CGI

A dark 3D tower with a crane could advertise any real-estate or construction product. Vera's proof is paperwork that doesn't add up, and the hero doesn't show any.

Fix: Show the mechanism in the first viewport: three copies of one delivery, with the gap stamped on them.

P2

Two primary actions, and the wrong one leads

The hero's filled button is "Watch the demo", while the nav's filled button is "Free leak check". The leak check is the offer the whole page builds toward.

Fix: "Book a free leak check" is the only filled button. Second action: "Send a challan on WhatsApp".

P2

Nothing for a careful buyer to check

There's no customer proof, pilot result, pricing signal, data-security answer, FAQ, privacy or terms page, or company address. The team section shows only headshots and business-school names. For a buyer handing over purchase orders and bills, this is where trust is won or lost.

Fix: Add Proof, Security, FAQ and a founders' note (see the blueprint below).

P3

Small things

The theme toggle on a marketing page adds a choice nobody needs. The desks are numbered 01–04 even though they aren't a sequence (the rollout is, and should keep its numbers). The material marquee repeats the same list twice. Selection color, scrollbars and focus rings are browser defaults.

Keep these

A redesign replaces the look. It doesn't throw away what's working. These carry over unchanged.

  • The trade vocabulary: OPC 53, Fe500 TMT, RMC M25, hindrance register, RA bills, BOQ. Competitors can't fake it.
  • "18 bags never reached the store. 41 bags issued, not used." It's the best line on the site, and it should be the hero.
  • The offer: free first, no new app, one site, one rupee figure, and each desk has to pay for itself before the next.
  • WhatsApp and voice notes in the supervisor's own language. That's the real moat. Show it, don't just say it.
  • "Vera is Latin for 'true'." It's a quiet brand line. Keep it in the footer and the founders' note.
  • The technical basics: lazy images, alt text, reduced motion, no sideways scroll, and a page under 1.1 MB.

Adding Dubai and the UAE

The mechanism is the same. The paperwork, currency, languages and contracts are different. The homepage should feel local in both markets, not like an Indian page with a dirham sign added.

India: who it's for

  • Mid-size building and EPC contractors doing ₹50–500 Cr a year, with 4–20 live sites
  • Owner-led firms in Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities, with accounts in Tally and registers on paper
  • Supervisors on WhatsApp in Hindi, Marathi and other regional languages
  • Buyers: the owner or MD, plus the head of projects or QS

UAE: who it's for

  • Mid-size main contractors and subcontractors (civil, MEP, fit-out) doing about AED 25–250M a year, roughly the same band as India. Check this figure.
  • 5–30 live sites across Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi, with many owners of Indian or Pakistani origin
  • Supervisors and crews sending voice notes in Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Tamil, Bengali or Tagalog, with client documents in English and Arabic
  • FIDIC-style contracts, monthly interim payment applications certified by the consultant, and 10% retention
  • Buyers: the MD or GM, plus the commercial manager or QS

How to get there

The order of work with the Impeccable skill, plus the materials only your team can provide. Real photos and documents are most of what makes a site feel expensive.

  1. Write down the product truth for both markets

    /impeccable init creates PRODUCT.md with both ICPs, the offer, and the claims you can actually back up.

  2. Lock in Direction A and plan the page

    /impeccable shape homepage, using the blueprint above. Then build the hero and the leak section first, since they carry the story.

  3. Build the rest from the blueprint

    Desks switcher, how it works, two markets, security, FAQ, close. Each one is built from real documents, never from placeholder icons.

  4. Check, then finish

    /impeccable audit to get contrast, touch targets and performance to 18/20 or better, then /impeccable polish. The target is a hero visible in under 1.5 s on a mid-range Android phone over 4G.

What your team needs to supply

  • 5–10 real challans, delivery notes and register pages, with names and prices redacted
  • Real pilot results and permission to name customers, or an honest "first pilots running"
  • Site photos in India and Dubai, if you choose Direction B now or later
  • The UAE WhatsApp Business number, the Dubai address and the trade-licence entity name
  • Data-hosting facts for the security section, and privacy and terms reviewed by counsel
  • A refined wordmark. The current "vera ai" mark is Geist with a custom V, and should be drawn properly
