## Platform

web

## Users

Mid-size construction contractors in two markets:

- **India:** firms doing ₹50–500 Cr of work a year, whose numbers still live in WhatsApp chats, paper registers and one overworked accounts team.
- **UAE / Dubai:** contractors in the comparable band (inferred: roughly AED 20–200 M a year), working with multilingual site crews, LPOs, delivery notes and IPCs. The confirmed fact is only that the UAE is a second target market. Size band and vocabulary are inferences to confirm.

Three people must act on the homepage (confirmed):

- **Owner / MD:** signs off on tools and spend.
- **CFO / finance head:** cares about leakage, billing cycles and cash collection.
- **Project director / ops:** runs sites, supervisors, stores and mobilisation.

## Product Purpose

Vera is AI software set up around a contractor's sites. Supervisors send voice notes, photos and challans to one WhatsApp number, in Hindi or their own language. Vera writes the true daily record of each site from them. It matches purchase orders, delivery challans, store issues and measured work against the BOQ, to show where material and money leak.

Success: an owner, CFO or project director starts the free Material Leak Check.

## Positioning

Every register looks fine on its own. The leak only shows when you match them. Vera builds the matched record from what sites already send on WhatsApp, with no new app, and proves the leak in rupees on one site, free, before anyone signs anything. "Vera" is Latin for "true".

## Operating Context

Source documents:

- India: purchase orders, delivery challans, stock or store register, BOQ, RA (running account) bills, hindrance register, cube test certificates, Tally or ERP exports.
- UAE equivalents (to confirm): LPO, delivery note, store issue voucher, BOQ, IPC or interim payment application.

Daily rituals:

- Supervisors send voice notes and photos from site.
- Owners receive an 8 AM one-page summary across every site.
- Teams produce a weekly claim-ready PDF and a monthly Tally or ERP import file.

Materials: cement OPC 53, Fe500 TMT steel, RMC M25, shuttering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

There are four desks, all running on one WhatsApp number and one daily record:

1. **Material Leak Check:** free, day 1. Matches three months of POs, challans, issues and BOQ line by line. Delivers a one-page money-leaking summary, inventory balancing across sites, and wastage by supervisor.
2. **One-Day Mobilisation:** for developers starting new sites. Cuts the contractor set-up inspection from 2–3 days to one, with certificates checked on WhatsApp beforehand.
3. **Site Record Desk:** core, monthly. Daily progress against plan, labour by contractor, materials in, hindrance register. Produces the 8 AM summary, a weekly claim PDF and a Tally or ERP file.
4. **Bill-to-Cash Desk:** add-on, from month 2–3. Builds running-bill packs and follows up until paid. It cannot speed up a government treasury.

Constraints:

- **Rollout:** one desk at a time. The target is 90% of supervisors reporting daily.
- **Primary action:** Free Material Leak Check (confirmed). WhatsApp is the secondary path.

## Product Principles

1. The record is the product: every surface shows one true daily record, not features.
2. Show the rupees first: proof in money, on one site, before any ask.
3. Meet sites where they are: WhatsApp, voice and the supervisor's own language, with no new app.
4. Earn the next desk: one desk at a time, each paying for the last.
