# Homepage world derivation (new-work-world steps 1–3)

## 1. Mechanism, scene, rut
- Mechanism: four registers (PO, challan, store issue, measured BOQ) each look fine alone; Vera matches them from WhatsApp inputs and the leak appears only in the match, in rupees.
- Scene: owner/MD or CFO at a desk in Pune, Indore or Dubai at 8 AM, phone first then laptop; project director between sites; supervisors on site sending voice notes.
- Cultural home: Indian and Gulf mid-size contracting: drawings, BOQs, RA bills/IPCs, CAs and auditors, Tally, WhatsApp, IIM-trained founders.
- Must prove: the leak is real, findable, priced in ₹/AED, from what sites already send, free.
- Rut (excluded): construction-tech SaaS default (hard-hat orange, dark site video, dashboard mock, card grid); its predictable opposite (cream editorial broadsheet: the current newspaper intro); the name's literal reading (truth seals, stamps, the challan book = rejected "Triplicate").

## 2. Seven candidates (by resonance)
1. Engineering drawing set (notation): GA sheet, title block, structural grid bubbles, dimension strings, revision clouds. Carries the mechanism as a dimension chain that fails to close. Ink on white, one red for the open dimension; technical lettering.
2. Audit working papers (document): tick-mark legend, cross-referenced schedules, "agreed to PO". CFO language. Graphite on white, green audit pencil.
3. Indian modernist concrete and brick (place/material): Kahn's IIM Ahmedabad, Doshi, Chandigarh; board-formed concrete, brick, hard sun and shadow, circular voids. Monumental grotesk.
4. Security printing (print): banknote guilloche, intaglio, serial numbers: "every rupee accounted for". Deep ink green or slate, fine line work.
5. Metro wayfinding identity (identity/data graphic): Delhi Metro and Dubai Metro line diagrams; four registers as lines, sites as stations, the leak as a divergence. Enamel white, signal colours.
6. Commodity rate board (screen/data): cement and steel rate sheets, market terminals. Black and amber, tabular figures. Near the dark-neon default.
7. Site hoarding (place/print): large-format wraps around Mumbai and Dubai sites. Printed vinyl, project render, developer colour.
Families: notation/document (1, 2), place/material (3, 7), print (4), identity/data (5, 6). Contractor-operated tools: none used.

## 3. Directions
- A. "General Arrangement": the homepage as a drawing sheet set; the hero is a dimension chain that does not close, the gap dimensioned in ₹.
- B. "Kahn's Light": concrete and brick, sun and shadow, monumental type; the record as structure.
- C. "Line Diagram": metro-map identity; four register lines converge per site, the leak breaks the line.
